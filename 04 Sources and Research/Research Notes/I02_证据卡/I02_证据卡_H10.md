# B 适用范围
管谁: "means a person who, either alone or jointly or in common with other persons, controls the collection, holding, processing or use of the data"（资料使用者（脚注 2 引《私隐条例》第 2(1) 条的定义））
管什么: "A data breach is generally regarded as a suspected or actual breach of the security of personal data held by a data user"（资料外泄的处理和通报）
触发条件: "after becoming aware of the data breach"（知悉资料外泄）

# C 规定位置清单
| 条目 | 段落或条款号（原件 PDF 页码） | 逐字引文 | 该段在讲什么 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| H10-C1 | 第 1 页 · What is a data breach? | A data breach is generally regarded as a suspected or actual breach of the security of personal data held by a data user | 资料外泄的定义，包括“怀疑”外泄。 |
| H10-C2 | 第 2 页 · 第 1 段 | A data breach may amount to a contravention of Data Protection Principle (DPP) 4(1) and (2) in Schedule 1 to the PDPO. | 外泄可能违反保障资料第 4(1)、(2) 原则。 |
| H10-C3 | 第 2 页 · Cyberattacks | organisations may fall prey to cyberattacks (e.g., ransomware, | 外泄的常见原因之一是网络攻击，例子包括勒索软件。 |
| H10-C4 | 第 6 页 · Step 4 第 2 段 | In general, the data user should notify the PCPD and the affected data subjects as soon as practicable after becoming aware of the data breach, | 一般应在知悉后尽快通知公署和受影响的资料当事人。 |
| H10-C5 | 第 6 页 · Step 4 第 2 段 | particularly if the data breach is likely to result in a real risk of harm to those affected data subjects | 尤其是可能造成实际损害风险时。 |
| H10-C6 | 第 6 页 · 脚注 4 | The guidelines promulgated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority about reporting data breaches apply to authorised institutions. | 认可机构还要遵守金管局的通报指引。 |
| H10-C7 | 第 7 页 · To whom should the notification be given? | decide whether any of the following parties should be notified as soon as practicable | 要考虑通知的对象：受影响当事人、公署、其他执法机构、有关监管机构、其他能采取补救措施的人。 |
| H10-C8 | 第 8 页 · When to notify? | should generally be given as soon as practicable after becoming aware of the incident, regardless of the progress of any internal investigation | 知悉后尽快通报，不必等内部调查做完。 |
| H10-C9 | 第 8 页 · When to notify? | After the full details of the incident are revealed, all information should be submitted to the PCPD and other law enforcement agencies without delay. | 查清全部细节后，不延误地把全部信息交给公署和其他执法机构。 |
| H10-C10 | 第 8 页 · How to notify? | If a data breach results in particularly serious harm or affects a large number of individuals, using multiple methods to publicise the breach | 损害特别严重或受影响人数多时，可以考虑通过多种渠道公布；同一句后半，原文说这是“a reasonable approach”。 |
| H10-C11 | 第 8 页 · Notification to the PCPD | Oral notifications are not accepted. | 向公署通报不接受口头方式；建议用通报表格（H13）。 |

# F 误读预警
| 误读位置 | 预警内容 |
| :--- | :--- |
| 全文 | 容易被写成什么：指引写明“通报不是强制的”。<br>原文实际是什么：指引没有这句话；“not mandatory under the Ordinance”写在通报表格 H13 里。 |
