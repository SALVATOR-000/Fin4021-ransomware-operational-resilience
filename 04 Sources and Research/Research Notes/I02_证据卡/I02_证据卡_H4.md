# B 适用范围
管谁: "All Authorized Institutions"（所有认可机构（收件人一栏））
管什么: "effective incident response and management capability and procedures to deal with significant incidents"（应对重大事故的能力和程序，以及对外沟通的原则）
触发条件: "whenever any significant incident occurs"（发生任何重大事故）

# C 规定位置清单
| 条目 | 段落或条款号（原件 PDF 页码） | 逐字引文 | 该段在讲什么 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| H4-C1 | 扫描件第 1 页 · 正文第 2 段 | It is nevertheless important for all AIs to ensure that whenever any significant incident occurs, they take the actions set out below | 适用于任何重大事故，不限于网络事故；通函没有定义“significant incident”。 |
| H4-C2 | 扫描件第 1 页 · Immediate incident response | The AI concerned should immediately analyse the cause of the incident and as soon as practicable rectify or contain the problem. | 立即分析原因，尽快修复或控制问题。 |
| H4-C3 | 扫描件第 1 页 · Immediate incident response | The top priority should be to safeguard the interests of customers who have been or may be affected by the incident. | 首要任务是保障已受影响或可能受影响客户的利益。 |
| H4-C4 | 扫描件第 1 页 · Customer notifications | The AI should proactively notify the customers affected or likely to be affected through the most effective means | 主动通知已受影响和可能受影响的客户。 |
| H4-C5 | 扫描件第 1 页 · Customer notifications | advise them of the steps or precautionary measures that they need to take as well as whether the bank would reimburse any losses | 通知内容：客户要采取的措施，以及银行是否赔偿。 |
| H4-C6 | 扫描件第 1–2 页 · Customer notifications | Where necessary, the AI should also notify other affected AIs (so that they can in turn notify their affected customers) as soon as practicable. | 有需要时尽快通知其他受影响的银行，由它们再通知自己的客户。 |
| H4-C7 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Public announcement (a) | where the nature of the incident is serious, for example if the incident entails the disruption of any essential and critical banking service channel | 考虑公开公告的情形之一：事故性质严重，例如基本而关键的银行服务渠道中断。 |
| H4-C8 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Public announcement (b) | where a large number of customers have been or may be affected, | 考虑公开公告的情形之二：大量客户已受或可能受影响。 |
| H4-C9 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Public announcement | In notifying the customers and making a public announcement, the AI concerned should include the key facts of the incident | 通知和公告要写事故的关键事实。 |
| H4-C10 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Public announcement | whether a public announcement may prejudice any ongoing criminal proceedings or any investigation | 公告前要考虑会不会妨碍刑事程序或调查。 |
| H4-C11 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Reporting incident to the HKMA | once an AI has become aware that a significant incident has occurred, the AI concerned should notify the HKMA immediately | 知悉发生重大事故后立即通报金管局。 |
| H4-C12 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Reporting incident to the HKMA | provide it with whatever information is available at the time | 通报时提供当时掌握的任何信息。 |
| H4-C13 | 扫描件第 2 页 · Reporting incident to the HKMA | an AI should not wait until it has rectified the problem before reporting the incident to the HKMA | 不应等到问题修复后才向金管局通报。 |
| H4-C14 | 扫描件第 3 页 · Handling of customers’ and media’s enquiry | deploy swiftly adequate resources and communication channels (e.g. customer service hotlines and extended office hours of branch services) | 迅速调配热线、延长分行服务时间等资源处理查询。 |
| H4-C15 | 扫描件第 3 页 · Handling of customers’ and media’s enquiry | copies of public communication materials should be submitted to the HKMA for reference | 对外沟通材料要抄送金管局备考。 |
