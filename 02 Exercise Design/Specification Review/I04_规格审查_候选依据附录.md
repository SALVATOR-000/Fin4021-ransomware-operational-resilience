## Q05a（LC · C02）· 初查：拆分：立场未找到；法律闸门有条件

- R16c（Cap. 455） · 第 83 页 · s.25A(1)(c)：“an indictable offence, he shall as soon as it is reasonable for him to do so disclose that knowledge or suspicion”
- R16d（Cap. 575） · 第 20 页 · s.8(a)：“to or for the benefit of a person knowing that, or being reckless as to whether, the person is a terrorist or terrorist associate”
- R16d（Cap. 575） · 第 9 页 · s.2(1) 'terrorist act' para (a)(ii)(B)：“(B) made for the purpose of advancing a political, religious or ideological cause;”
- 留给专业判断（检索代理原话）：No provision in the whitelist permits, prohibits or sets conditions for paying a ransom. The posture itself (pay / do not pay / defer) is professional judgment. DIRECT applies only to the 'legal checks' half of the question. These statutory gates bite only on facts: (a) OSCO s.25A(1): disclosure to an 'authorized officer' (s.2: any police officer) if the bank knows or suspects property 'was used in connection with' or 'is intended to be used in connection with' an indictable offence. Whether a ransom transfer falls in that wording is a legal judgment; s.25A(2)(a) prior-disclosure-plus-consent gate applies only to acts within s.25(1). (b) UNATMO s.8(a): no property to a known or recklessly-suspected terrorist or associate except under licence; s.12(1) disclosure duty. A profit-motivated ransomware actor is not a 'terrorist' unless the s.2 political/religious/ideological limb is met, and attribution is unverified (line 100). The whitelist does not contain the designation lists (UNATMO s.4 notices), Cap. 537, AMLO, or the Theft Ordinance; L3 Sch. 1 (separate 2025 file) lists 'blackmail' only as a 'specified offence'. Group may prefer to split this unit: posture = NONE, gates = DIRECT-conditional.
- **本卡发给 LC 的前情**：没有。
- **回查提示（I05）**：
  - 白名单里没有任何条文允许、禁止或限制支付赎金；四部条例全文都没有“ransom”一词。付款立场本身属专业判断。
  - 法律闸门翻这几处：Cap. 455 第 25 条（第 82 页，处理犯罪得益）、第 25A 条（第 83 页，披露）；Cap. 575 第 7、8 条（第 20 页）、第 12 条（第 36 页）。
  - 容易看错：第 25A(1) 条的条件是“knows or suspects”，时间是“as soon as it is reasonable”；第 25A(2) 条是披露后行事的免责，不是付款许可。Cap. 575 第 8 条要“知道或罔顾”对方是恐怖分子或与其有联系者，而卡上攻击者身份一直未核实。
